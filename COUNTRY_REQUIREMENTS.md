# Country and Document Requirements

## Important status

The entries in `src/document-rules.js` are an initial research registry, **not a production-certified requirements database**. They must not be treated as definitive or used to promise acceptance.

Requirements vary by country, document type, application channel (online or printed), applicant age and current government policy. A passport photo rule must not automatically be reused for a visa, residence permit or other ID.

## Current registry coverage

The code currently contains initial entries for:
- India — child passport route.
- United States — passport and visa routes.
- United Kingdom — passport and visa routes.
- Canada — passport route.
- Australia — passport route.

This list describes code entries only. It does not certify that every dimension, file-size range, background, framing instruction or editing restriction is accurate and current.

## Verification required before launch

For every exact route, record:
- Stable route ID and document name.
- Digital, printed or both submission modes.
- Exact pixel/mm dimensions and permitted file-size range.
- Background, head-size, framing and expression requirements.
- Whether cropping, background replacement or any other edit is prohibited.
- Official government source URL and quoted/parsed source evidence.
- Date checked, reviewer and next review date.

Use the official government application page and current instructions, not search snippets or third-party blogs. If a rule is unclear or conflicting, mark the route **unverified** and disable compliance claims and official-use export guidance for it.

## Product wording

Say “helps prepare a photo using the listed guidance,” not “guaranteed accepted.” Always advise users to check the live official instructions for their application.
